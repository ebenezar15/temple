God’s initial plan: Eden was meant to fill the earth

The garden was a starting point, not the finish line. Step through the expansion of God’s presence.

God’s initial plan was never to take people out of the world into heaven. It was to bring heaven into the world through people. Adam was a priest in the sanctuary and a king over creation, sent to extend the garden until the whole earth was God’s temple.

The fall interrupted the mission but never cancelled it. Christ, the last Adam (1 Cor 15:45), has restarted it. Sons and daughters now carry it as kings and priests until the earth is “filled with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD” (Hab 2:14).
