Part five · a detailed study

Prophets, the Fivefold and the Kingdom Now

Israel’s prophets guarded the covenant and held kings and priests to it. In the new covenant Christ gives five ministries to equip all his people to be kings and priests, not someday but now, in every sphere of life. Here is how that calling was narrowed, and why the original intent is bigger.

    Diagnosis · funnel

    How the calling was shrunk

    In much of the modern Western church, the calling to be kings and priests over all of life narrowed, layer by layer, to evangelism, discipleship classes and a ticket to heaven. Evangelism and discipleship are not the problem; shrinking everything to them is. Toggle to restore the original intent.

    Two ways of telling the gospel

    The same truths, but a different centre. One is a transaction; the other is a restored relationship that makes sons and daughters into heirs.

        Reading the “end” in its setting

        Jesus tied his warnings about the end to “this generation” and the fall of the Temple. Read that way, the old covenant age ended in AD 70, and we live in the age of the growing kingdom, which ends with Christ’s bodily return. Select an event.
