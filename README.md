# Where Heaven Meets Earth

An interactive, animated Bible study in ten parts:

- **Part I: The Garden-Temple.** Eden as God's sanctuary on his mountain, Adam as priest-king, the Tabernacle as a portable Eden, the Ark of the Covenant and its journey, Solomon's Temple, and the New Jerusalem as Eden fulfilled.
- **Part II: The Serpent and the Seed.** The Hebrew *nāḥāš* (serpent, diviner, shining one), why the serpent of Genesis 3 reads as a divine being inside God's sanctuary, Ezekiel 28's fallen guardian cherub and the high priest's breastpiece, Isaiah 14's "Day Star", seraphim as burning serpents, the curse decoded, the war of the two seeds from Genesis 3:15 to Revelation 12, the bronze serpent and John 3:14, and "that ancient serpent".
- **Part III: Babel, Babylon and the City of God.** The Tower of Babel as a Mesopotamian ziggurat, the three layers of Babel's rebellion, the *shem* ("name") wordplay, the seventy nations of Deuteronomy 32, Bethel's "gate of heaven", Pentecost as Babel reversed, and the two cities from Cain to Revelation.
- **Part IV: Kings and Priests** (a detailed study). God's design for humanity as priest-kings, how kingship and priesthood were twisted, why Israel's offices were separated, roles, access and authority, kings who crossed the line, a map of kings, priests and those who were both from Adam to the church, the order of Melchizedek, converging promises, and how believers are called to function as a royal priesthood, with a study guide and memory verses. It also includes **Supply and Support**, interactive sermon notes on Isaiah 3: supply and support, the signs of judgment, Babel or Bethel, heaven came down, worship, gatekeepers and gossip, the orphan generation, the three Babylons, a generation of Daniels, and one accord.
- **Part V: Prophets, the Fivefold and the Kingdom Now** (a detailed study). The Old Testament prophetic office and its relationships with kings and priests, the five ministries of Ephesians 4 and how each equips believers as kings and priests, what changed between the covenants, why the royal priesthood is a present calling, how the church narrowed that calling, the AD 70 setting of Jesus' end-time warnings, and objections answered.
- **Part VI: The Keys of the Kingdom** (a detailed study). What keys mean throughout the Bible, the key of the house of David and the royal steward, Caesarea Philippi, binding and loosing, doors opened in Acts, how the keys were narrowed to forgiveness alone, how to operate in them, and objections answered.
- **Part VII: Sowing and Reaping** (a detailed study). God's seed principle from Genesis 1 and 8 to the final harvest, eight laws of the harvest, the four soils, the principle traced through Scripture, Christ the seed that died, the connection to blessing, grace and the great exchange, why kingdom principles can feel like manipulation, and objections answered.
- **Part VIII: Spiritual Warfare and Dominion** (a detailed study). Ephesians 6:12 and the layered battlefield, authority (*exousia*), power (*dynamis*), dominion and strongholds, the chain of authority, the armour of God as God's own armour, what Scripture shows we may speak to and command, and binding and loosing ("shall have been bound in heaven"). It also covers **speaking and prophesying over our lives** (the power of declaration, a declaration builder, and declaration compared with positive thinking and witchcraft) and **breaking curses, witchcraft and bondages** (from the curse to "no more curse", Proverbs 26:2, an evidence map, the Bible's own verbs for "cancel" and "break", and a path to freedom). It adds **seated in the heavenly places** ("heavenly places" pictured as the sanctuary with a tabernacle walk-through; the holy place's lampstand, showbread and incense as the priestly privileges of illumination, fellowship and access; privileges, not powers; sit, walk, stand; every spiritual blessing of Ephesians 1:3–14; our dual reality; co-heirs with Christ and the *huios* difference) and **far above all** (the four power words of Ephesians 1:19, the five things Christ is above, "the feet are in the body", Psalm 110:1 "sit … until", every enemy to be put under his feet, death as the last enemy and what the body does about it now, living from above in daily life, and the body's assignment). Then come errors and extremes, sons, priests and kings in battle, Jesus' "It is written", a daily battle rhythm, and objections answered.
- **Part IX: Heaven and Earth United** (the capstone). Ephesians 1:10 and the heaven–earth overlap through the whole story, *anakephalaiōsis* (summing up all things under one head), how every part of the study fits, God's initial plan of Eden filling the earth, Romans 8 and the revealing of the sons of God, *nēpios*, *teknon* and *huios*, the body of Christ, what sons and daughters do as kings and priests, heaven in every sphere of life, and objections answered.
- **Part X: Renewing the Mind and Transformation** (a detailed study). Romans 12:2 word by word with a conformed/transformed metamorphosis animation, the Bible's vocabulary of the inner life, how a thought becomes a life (Romans 8:5–6), taking every thought captive, a lie-for-truth exchange tool, the means God uses to renew the mind, the Philippians 4:8 thought filter, 2 Corinthians 3:18 "from one degree of glory to another", moving from an orphan mindset to a son's, a daily renewal tracker, and objections answered.

## Interactive pieces

Flip-card clues · switchable "cosmic mountain" diagram (Eden, Sinai, Tabernacle, Temple, New Jerusalem) · walk-through Tabernacle plan · explorable Ark with an animated journey map · creation-as-temple table · animated menorah · God's-dwelling timeline · Genesis ↔ Revelation mirror · layered map of the ancient Near East (rivers of Eden, Shinar, Abraham's route, the exile, Pentecost nations) · build-a-ziggurat with a Genesis 11:5 zoom-out · to-scale height chart · Shem genealogy · seventy-nations animation · Jacob's stairway · Babel/Pentecost comparison slider · "mighty works of God" in 17 languages · two-cities timeline · Babylon vs New Jerusalem table · connections web · Venn diagram of *nāḥāš* · Ezekiel 28 verse walker with breastpiece gems · morning-star animation and Philippians 2 "two paths" chart · seraph/cherub illustration · the serpent's descent from God's mountain to the lake of fire · animated two-seeds timeline · "look and live" bronze serpent · 14-question quiz.

## Learning tools

- **Parts menu**: every part as a tile, each with a progress ring.
- **Study tools on every section**: ✓ Mark studied, ☆ Save and ✎ Note (notes save as you type).
- **My study** drawer: overall progress, a **Review** tab of memory-verse flashcards from every part, a searchable **Glossary** of Hebrew and Greek terms, "continue where you left off", saved sections, and all notes, which you can copy or download.
- **Your journey** map on the home page: one road through all the parts, with rings that fill as you study.
- **Check your understanding**: a short quiz at the end of each part, with your best score kept in My study.
- Progress, saves and notes are stored only in your browser on this device.

## Motion and navigation

- **Search** (`Ctrl/⌘ + K` or `/`): jump to any section of any page.
- **Shift + ← / →**: previous or next part.
- Animated page transitions, drifting light motes on banners, kinetic headings, staggered reveals, a section rail on wide screens, card spotlights and 3D tilt, magnetic buttons, click ripples, and celebration bursts on quiz answers and key moments.
- Everything respects the system "reduce motion" setting.

## Colour schemes and font

Use the **Colours** button (top right) to switch between seven schemes (Follow device, Eden Dawn, Tabernacle, Ishtar Lapis, Sinai Stone, New Jerusalem, Night Sky) or pick a custom highlight colour. Your choice is remembered in the browser.

Text is set in **Avenir Next**. Avenir Next is a commercial typeface: it is used automatically wherever it is installed (all Apple devices), and the page falls back to the free, similar Nunito Sans from Google Fonts elsewhere.

## Running it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. The pages share `assets/styles.css` and `assets/app.js`; the only network requests are Google Fonts and the public-domain artwork hot-linked from Wikimedia Commons (each image has a drawn fallback if it cannot load).

## Editing

Source lives in `src/`:

| File | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `src/pages/*.html` | Each page's sections and text |
| `src/partials/*.html` | Shared header (navigation, colour picker) and footer |
| `src/styles.css` | Colour schemes, layout and animation |
| `src/data.js` | Content for Parts I and III: clues, timelines, map coordinates, quiz, sources |
| `src/data-serpent.js` | Content for Part II: the serpent and the seed |
| `src/data-kings.js`, `src/app-kings.js` | Content and interactive code for Part IV: kings and priests |
| `src/data-supply.js`, `src/app-supply.js` | Part IV add-on: Supply and Support sermon notes |
| `src/fx.js` | Site-wide motion, search palette and interaction layer |
| `src/data-prophets.js`, `src/app-prophets.js` | Content and interactive code for Part V: prophets and the fivefold |
| `src/data-keys.js`, `src/app-keys.js` | Content and interactive code for Part VI: the keys of the kingdom |
| `src/data-sowing.js`, `src/app-sowing.js` | Content and interactive code for Part VII: sowing and reaping |
| `src/data-warfare.js`, `src/app-warfare.js` | Content and interactive code for Part VIII: spiritual warfare, declarations, curses |
| `src/data-sons.js`, `src/app-sons.js` | Content and interactive code for Part IX and the “seated with Christ” sections of Part VIII |
| `src/data-far.js`, `src/app-far.js` | Part VIII add-on: far above all, enemies under his feet, the last enemy |
| `src/data-mind.js`, `src/app-mind.js` | Content and interactive code for Part X: renewing the mind |
| `src/data-sanct.js`, `src/app-sanct.js` | Part VIII add-on: “heavenly places” as the sanctuary (light, bread, incense) |
| `src/study.js` | Learning layer: parts menu, progress, saves, notes, My study drawer, journey map |
| `src/app.js` | Interactive behaviour |

After editing, rebuild the pages:

```sh
node scripts/build.mjs
```
