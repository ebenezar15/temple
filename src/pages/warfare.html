The four words of Ephesians 6:12

Ephesians 6 uses wrestling language (palē): close, personal and hand to hand. Notice the verbs, though: “be strong in the Lord”, “put on”, “stand”, “withstand”, “pray”. The chapter never sends the church off to hunt the enemy. It tells the church to hold ground that Christ has already taken.

The powers are real but subordinate. They are created (Col 1:16), disarmed (Col 2:15), made subject to Christ (1 Pet 3:22), and shown God’s wisdom through the church (Eph 3:10).
