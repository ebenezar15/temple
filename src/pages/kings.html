Kings and Priests

Humanity was made to rule the world for God and to stand in God’s presence for the world. Follow that two-fold calling from Eden, through its twisting and its splitting in Israel, to the priest-king Jesus and the “royal priesthood” he makes of his people.

    The order of Melchizedek

    His name means “my king is righteousness”; he is king of Salem, “peace”. He appears once, without a recorded genealogy, and becomes the key to how a king from Judah can be a priest forever.
