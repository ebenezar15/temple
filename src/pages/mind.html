Renewing the Mind and Transformation

“Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewal of your mind” (Rom 12:2). This part covers how sons and daughters are changed from the inside out: the words Scripture uses, how a thought becomes a life, how to take thoughts captive and exchange lies for truth, and the means God uses to transform us “from one degree of glory to another”.

Beholding and becoming: from one degree of glory to another

Move from the veil to unveiled beholding, and watch the reflection grow brighter.

Transformation is not self-improvement. It is the Spirit making us like Jesus as we look at him. Moses’ face shone because he had been with God, but the glory faded. Ours increases, because the Spirit lives in us and the veil is gone.

This is how the sons of Part IX are revealed. Creation waits for people whose minds have been made new, who think heaven’s thoughts in earth’s places (Rom 8:19; Col 3:1–2).
