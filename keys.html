Culture · the royal steward

Inside the King’s house

In Judah the official “over the house” (ʾăšer ʿal-habbayit) was the king’s chief steward, something like a prime minister. A tomb inscription found at Silwan in Jerusalem names a royal steward with this very title, possibly Shebna himself. Explore the palace and what the key opened.

Matthew 16:13–20 · the setting

Keys beside the “gates of Hades”

Jesus led his disciples about 40 km north of Galilee to a place of pagan worship, under a cliff with a cave believed to lead to the underworld. There he spoke of his church, the gates of Hades and the keys. Explore the scene.
