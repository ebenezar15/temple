Cosmic geography

The mountain of God, in three rings

In the ancient Near East the gods lived on a cosmic mountain, the high centre of the world where life-giving water flowed out and heaven touched earth. Eden, Sinai and the Tabernacle share the same three zones of holiness. Switch between them.

East means away from GodExiles from God’s presence keep moving east: Adam and Eve are driven out on the east (Gen 3:24), Cain settles “east of Eden” (Gen 4:16), Babel’s builders migrate east to Shinar (Gen 11:2), and Lot chooses the east toward Sodom (Gen 13:11). The Tabernacle and Temple face east, so every worshipper walks west, back toward the presence.

The Ark of the Covenant

A gold-covered chest about 2.5 × 1.5 × 1.5 cubits (roughly 115 × 70 × 70 cm), the Ark sat in the Most Holy Place beneath two cherubim. David called it “the footstool of our God” (1 Chr 28:2). Explore its parts, then follow its journey on the map.

Man in the sanctuary

Adam: image, priest and king

Ancient kings set up statues of themselves to mark the lands they ruled, and temples were not finished until the god’s image was installed. Genesis says God placed his own living image, humanity, inside his garden-temple to serve, guard and rule.

Creation as a temple dedication

Tree of life → lampstand

A golden tree in the holy place

The menorah was hammered from one piece of pure gold, with branches, almond blossoms, buds and flowers (Exod 25:31–40). It stood just outside the veil like a stylised tree of life, burning in the presence of God.

Aaron’s staff, kept beside the Ark, “sprouted and put forth buds and produced blossoms, and it bore ripe almonds” (Num 17:8). Life springs up again near the throne.

In Revelation 1:12–20 the risen Christ walks among seven golden lampstands, his churches. In Revelation 22:2 the tree of life stands in the city again.

The Most Holy Place: a perfect cube

20 × 20 × 20 cubits, overlaid with pure gold (1 Kgs 6:20). Only the high priest entered, once a year.

The New Jerusalem: a cube of gold

“Its length and width and height are equal”, 12,000 stadia each (Rev 21:16), pure gold like glass. The whole city has become the Most Holy Place. “I saw no temple in the city, for its temple is the Lord God the Almighty and the Lamb” (Rev 21:22).
